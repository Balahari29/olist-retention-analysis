# LinkedIn post (draft)

74% of the bad reviews for late orders were written before the parcel even arrived.

I analysed ~100K orders from Olist, a Brazilian marketplace, to test whether late deliveries hurt customer
satisfaction. They do: late orders scored 2.02 stars lower than on-time ones.

But most of those reviews came from a survey sent while the customer was still waiting. Counting only reviews written
after delivery, the gap shrinks to 0.76 stars: still real, but a different problem.

So the fix isn't only faster delivery. It's asking at the right time, and telling customers about a delay before
they have to ask.

Two more findings:
• 97% of customers bought only once
• Late deliveries rise from 4.5% to 11.6% on routes over 1,500 km
